## Vim Commands 
- Press `i` to start editing
- Press `Esc` to stop editing
- Type `:w` and press Enter to save
- Type `:q` and press Enter to quit
- Type `:wq` and press Enter to save and quit
- Type `:q!` and press Enter to quit without Saving
